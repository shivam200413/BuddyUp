# 🏃‍♂️ Activity Buddy

**Find your tribe, right in your neighborhood.**

Have you ever wanted to go for a jog, play a quick game of cricket, or hit the gym, but realized all your friends were busy? **Activity Buddy** is a hyper-local community discovery platform built to solve that exact problem. 

Whether you're looking for a running partner on your usual route or need a "fourth" for a badminton match, Activity Buddy connects you with like-minded people in your immediate vicinity in real-time.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 📍 **Hyper-Local Discovery:** Find activities and people within a 2-5km radius of your current location.
* 🤝 **Real-Time Matchmaking:** Connect with others planning outdoor games or fitness activities *now*.
* 🗺️ **Privacy-First Mapping:** Integrated with **OpenStreetMap** (via FlutterMap) to keep your location data transparent and secure.
* 📱 **Smooth Experience:** A clean, responsive UI built with **Flutter** for both Android and iOS.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** [Flutter](https://flutter.dev/) & [Dart](https://dart.dev/)
- **Backend:** [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) (Authentication, Real-time Database)
- **Geospatial Logic:** [PostGIS](https://postgis.net/) for efficient location-based queries.
- **Maps:** [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) via `flutter_map`.
